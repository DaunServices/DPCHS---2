    Flies & Biting Midges (Agas) Control Overview

    Flies and biting midges (agas) are far more than a persistent nuisance; they are aggressive vectors capable of contaminating food supplies, spreading dangerous pathogens, and compromising the health and safety of your home or commercial establishment. At Daun Pest Control & Hygiene Services, we deploy advanced, target-specific eradication protocols designed to eliminate flying pest infestations at the root, ensuring a hygienic and pest-free environment across Selangor, Kuala Lumpur, and Peninsular Malaysia.

    Flying insects such as house flies, blow flies, fruit flies, drain flies, and tiny biting midges (agas) thrive in environments marked by organic waste, moisture, and high humidity. Beyond causing painful bites and skin irritation—particularly from midges—these pests mechanically transmit dangerous pathogens like Salmonella and E. coli by moving between unsanitary areas and food preparation surfaces. Our integrated fly and agas management program combines thorough habitat inspection, targeted larviciding, and versatile professional control methods to break the breeding cycle and protect your property long-term.

    Core Treatment Focus

    • Targeted Breeding Site Elimination: Locating and treating organic sludge, stagnant water, damp soil, and waste accumulation zones where fly and agas larvae develop.
    • Surface Spraying: Applying effective residual insecticides on walls, window frames, fences, and resting spots to eliminate insects on contact and provide long-lasting protection.
    • ULV Misting: Utilizing ultra-low volume technology to generate fine, cold mists that drift through the air, effectively treating indoor spaces like offices, commercial kitchens, and factories without excessive moisture.
    • Conventional Fogging & Water-Based Fogging:
      • Conventional Fogging: Heavy smoke application for large outdoor areas to quickly knock down adult flying insect populations.
      • Water-Based Fogging: A modern innovation utilizing water instead of diesel oil. It is eco-friendly, free of pungent diesel odors and thick smoke, and exceptionally safe for surrounding buildings and residential communities.
    • Eco-Hygiene Sanitization: Recommending and implementing sanitation measures to eliminate organic build-up within drains, grease traps, and waste disposal areas.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    • What are the primary health risks associated with flies and agas infestations?
      Flies are notorious carriers of pathogenic bacteria, transferring germs from garbage and decaying matter directly onto exposed food items. Meanwhile, biting midges (agas) inflict painful, itchy bites that can lead to secondary skin infections and severe allergic reactions in sensitive individuals.
    • Why do agas and flies keep returning even after DIY spraying?
      Over-the-counter aerosols only kill adult insects currently active in the air, leaving hidden breeding sources like damp organic matter, stagnant water, and drain sludge untouched. Daun Pest Control identifies and eliminates these underlying breeding grounds permanently using professional techniques.
    • What is the difference between water-based fogging and conventional fogging?
      Conventional fogging uses diesel oil as a carrier, which produces a strong odor and oily smoke. In contrast, water-based fogging uses water as a medium, making it cleaner, odorless, eco-friendly, and much more comfortable for residential areas and food premises.
    • Are spraying and ULV misting methods safe for indoor spaces?
      Yes. Surface spraying is formulated for specific landing zones on exterior walls or hidden corners, while ULV misting produces a safe cold fog ideal for enclosed indoor areas without fire risks from oil or overly toxic fumes.
    • How should I prepare my premises before a fly and agas treatment?
      You must ensure that all drinks and food are covered and safely stored to prevent contamination while the pest control treatment is underway. Additionally, clear your countertops and keep pets in a secure, untreated room. Our team will provide a clear checklist prior to your scheduled service.
