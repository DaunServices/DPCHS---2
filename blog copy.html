      Effective Tips to Prevent Mice and Rats from Entering Your Home

      The presence of rodents in your home is not only a nuisance to your daily comfort, but it also poses a serious health threat to your family due to dangerous diseases like leptospirosis and salmonella. Additionally, these pests love to chew on electrical wiring, which can potentially trigger short circuits or household fires. To safeguard your living space, Daun Pest Control And Hygiene Services shares strategic and practical steps to cut off rodent access to your home.

      1. Seal Entry Points and Gaps

      Rodents possess remarkable body flexibility and can squeeze through holes as small as a coin. Regularly inspect your entire home, paying close attention to foundations, ventilation grilles, gaps beneath doors, and wall cracks. Tightly seal all openings using wire mesh, concrete, or silicone sealant so that rodents cannot chew their way through.

      2. Maintain Cleanliness and Waste Management

      Food sources are the primary magnet that attracts rodents to nest indoors. Make sure to dispose of kitchen waste daily into an outdoor trash can with a tightly fitted lid. Store food items like rice, sugar, and flour in thick plastic or glass airtight containers. Never leave food scraps or dirty dishes piling up overnight in the sink.

      3. Clean Up Yards and Outdoor Areas

      Overgrown lawns and dense vegetation often serve as ideal hiding spots for rodents before they break into your home. Routinely trim wild grass, keep firewood piles or stored clutter away from exterior walls, and ensure tree branches do not hang too close to roofs or upper-floor windows where they can be used as bridges.

      4. Utilize Natural Repellents

      As an extra layer of prevention, you can use scents that rodents strongly dislike, such as peppermint essential oil, cloves, or placing bitter cucumber slices in vulnerable corners. The strong aroma of these ingredients effectively drives them away from the area.

      5. Professional Pest Control Assistance

      If the rodent population in your home becomes uncontrollable or nests in hard-to-reach places, DIY methods are often insufficient.
