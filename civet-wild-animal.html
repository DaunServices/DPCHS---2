      Request a Wildlife Consultation

      Daun Pest Control And Hygiene Services offers professional solutions for managing wildlife intrusions, such as civet cats (musang) and stray animals in residential or commercial properties. We prioritize human safety while ensuring ethical animal handling.

      Overview

      Wildlife intrusions like civet cats cause structural damage and noise disruptions. Beyond property damage, these animals can carry ticks, mites, and harmful pathogens. Our integrated wildlife management ensures safe removal and protects your premises.

      Core Treatment Focus

      Our systematic intervention strategies ensure safe and humane resolution:

      • Safe Trapping: Humane trapping systems designed to secure animals without injury.
      • Targeted Intervention: Expert deployment when animals threaten property or safety.
      • Natural Habitat Release: Transporting captured wildlife safely away from urban spaces.
      • Health Risk Mitigation: Neutralizing secondary vectors like ticks and pathogens in nesting areas.

      Our Service Advantages

      Choosing us guarantees top-tier standards:

      • Humane Approach: Ethical relocation protecting animal welfare.
      • Experienced Specialists: Skilled field operatives trained in practical handling, safety protocols, and efficient wildlife management.
      • Comprehensive Care: End-to-end relocation, waste cleaning, and sanitization.
      Frequently Asked Questions

      Find answers regarding our wildlife and animal removal procedures.

      • Does this service harm the captured civet cats or strays?
        No. We use humane trapping and handle all animals with care without cruelty.
      • When should I call for wildlife control?
        Contact us as soon as animals like civet cats enter your roof or create safety concerns.
      • What risks are posed by civet cats and strays?
        They carry ticks, mites, and germs with potential exposure to diseases.
      • Where do the animals go after capture?
        They are transported and released safely into protected natural habitats.
      • Is area sanitization provided after removal?
        Yes, we sanitize the area to clear waste, remove odors, and eliminate parasites.
