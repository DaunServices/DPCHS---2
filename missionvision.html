FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Have Any Questions on
Your Mind?
Get In Touch
  • How long does the treatment process take?
    Most standard treatments take between 30 to 90 minutes, depending on the size of the property and the severity of the infestation.
  • How often should pest control services be scheduled?
    The ideal frequency for pest control services depends on the type of property, the level of infestation, and the surrounding environment. Generally, quarterly services are recommended.
  • Do you offer emergency pest control services?
    Yes, we provide emergency pest control services to handle urgent infestations quickly and safely.
  • Will I need to leave my home during treatment?
    In most cases, you don't need to leave. However, for certain intensive treatments, we may advise staying out for a few hours for your safety.
  • How soon will I see results after treatment?
    You will usually notice a significant drop in pest activity within 24 to 48 hours, though complete eradication may take a few days.
