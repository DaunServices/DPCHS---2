    1. Overview

    Daun Pest Control And Hygiene Services delivers specialized, rigorous restaurant pest management solutions designed specifically to protect food safety, maintain strict health authority compliance, and safeguard your establishment’s reputation. Pests like rodents, cockroaches, and flies pose an immediate threat to commercial kitchens, making proactive, continuous defense essential.

    A single pest sighting can permanently damage a restaurant's standing, trigger costly health department shutdowns, and ruin customer trust. Our commercial restaurant pest program provides discreet, reliable, and powerful protection tailored to the unique operational demands of food and beverage establishments across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor. We implement customized Integrated Pest Management (IPM) protocols that target the root causes of infestations—focusing on exclusion, sanitation guidance, and regulated treatments—ensuring your kitchen, dining area, and storage spaces remain completely spotless and compliant with all food safety regulations.

    2. Core Treatment Focus

    • Targeted Rodent Management: Strategic deployment of tamper-resistant exterior bait stations and interior monitoring traps to eliminate mice and rats before they compromise dry storage or food prep zones.
    • Advanced Cockroach Elimination: Precision gel baiting and non-repellent residual applications applied safely in hidden voids, behind heavy kitchen equipment, and around plumbing lines where German and American cockroaches breed.
    • Fly and Drain Control: Comprehensive biological treatments for floor drains and grease traps combined with targeted insect light trap (ILT) management to eradicate fruit flies, drain flies, and house flies at the source.
    • Perimeter and Structural Exclusion: Sealing entry points, gaps around utility lines, and door sweeps to block pests from ever crossing your threshold.

    3. Our Service Advantages

    • HACCP & Industry Compliant: Fully aligned with food safety standards, utilizing documentation and safe application methods approved for commercial food handling environments.
    • Discreet, After-Hours Scheduling: Service visits coordinated around your operating hours to ensure zero disruption to your daily dining service and staff workflow.
    • Proactive Prevention Focus: Regular monitoring, detailed digital reporting, and actionable sanitation recommendations to stop pest issues before they start.
    • Experienced Local Technicians: Licensed professionals with extensive expertise handling unique pest pressures and environmental conditions specific to food establishments in Malaysia.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    • 1. How often should a restaurant receive pest control treatments?
      Commercial kitchens require a minimum of monthly preventative service due to the constant presence of food, warmth, and moisture. High-risk facilities or those experiencing active pest pressure may benefit from bi-weekly or customized weekly maintenance visits.
    • 2. Are your treatments safe to use around food preparation areas?
      Yes. We strictly utilize food-safe, low-toxicity products applied selectively in non-food contact zones, wall voids, and hidden anchor points. All treatments comply with regulatory safety standards to ensure zero risk of chemical contamination.
    • 3. Will the pest control treatment disrupt our restaurant's operating hours?
      No. We schedule all commercial interventions during your off-hours—such as late nights or early mornings before prep begins—allowing treatments to settle completely before your staff and customers arrive.
    • 4. What immediate steps should we take before the technician arrives?
      Ensure all food items are tightly sealed and stored off the floor, thoroughly clean prep counters and equipment to remove loose grease or food debris, and clear direct access to plumbing lines, floor drains, and waste disposal areas.
    • 5. What if the restaurant faces a severe infestation of German cockroaches and rodents? Does the premises need to be closed, and how long after the treatment can operations resume?
      For severe infestations involving resilient German cockroaches and high rodent pressure, a temporary closure is highly recommended during intensive treatment to ensure maximum effectiveness and safety. We advise a minimum closure of 12 hours, with 24 hours being optimal. This extended window allows the treatment agents to settle fully, maximizes eradication rates, and gives your team ample time to complete a thorough surface wipe-down before reopening for business.
