Overview

Cockroaches are among the most persistent and resilient household and commercial pests, capable of triggering allergies, spreading pathogens, and contaminating food supplies. At Daun Pest Control And Hygiene Services, we deliver targeted, science-backed cockroach management solutions designed to eradicate infestations at their source. Whether you are dealing with the common German cockroach in a commercial kitchen or larger American cockroaches in a residential property, our licensed technicians combine advanced diagnostics with safe, industry-compliant treatments to restore complete sanitation and peace of mind to your environment.

Core Treatment Focus
  • Inspection and Identification: Pinpointing harboring sites, moisture sources, and entry points using specialized flushing agents and thermal tools.
  • Targeted Gel Baiting System: Deploying high-grade, non-repellent gel baits precisely into cracks, crevices, and hinges that foraging cockroaches consume and share with the colony, ensuring total elimination.
  • Targeted Monitoring System (Trap): Placing sticky traps and multi-catch monitoring stations in strategic high-risk zones to track cockroach activity levels, identify specific harboring hotspots, and measure the ongoing success of the treatment.
  • Residual Insecticide Applications: Spraying specialized micro-encapsulated liquid treatments along baseboards, cracks, crevices, and dark voids to create a protective barrier.
  • ULV Cold Misting Method: Utilizing Ultra-Low Volume (ULV) cold fogging to disperse a fine mist of micro-droplets into the air and hard-to-reach spaces, rapidly knocking down active adult flying or exposed crawling cockroaches in large or heavily infested areas.
  • Sanitation and Exclusion Advisory: Providing expert recommendations on sealing cracks, managing waste effectively, and eliminating moisture to prevent future rebounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long does a cockroach treatment take and when can I re-enter the property?
    Most residential treatments take between 45 to 90 minutes depending on the property size. You can typically re-enter the premises immediately after the application has dried and the area has settled, which usually takes about 30 to 60 minutes with proper ventilation.
  • Are the chemicals used by Daun Pest Control safe for children and pets?
    Yes. We prioritize human and environmental safety by using approved, low-toxicity formulations. Our technicians apply these products precisely into cracks, crevices, and secure stations away from direct contact areas.
  • Why do I still see cockroaches after the treatment?
    Seeing a few cockroaches shortly after treatment is normal. Our non-repellent baits and residual treatments are designed to attract pests so they ingest the poison and carry it back to the nest. It typically takes 7 to 14 days to see a total reduction in activity.
  • How can I prepare my home or office before the technicians arrive?
    We recommend clearing out food items from kitchen cabinets, sealing open food in plastic containers, cleaning up grease, and ensuring easy access to areas under sinks, behind refrigerators, and around plumbing lines.
  • How often should I schedule cockroach control services for my business?
    For commercial establishments such as restaurants, cafes, and food processing plants, we strongly recommend a monthly or bi-monthly preventive maintenance service to maintain compliance with health regulations and ensure a pest-free environment.
  • Is follow-up treatment necessary?
    Yes. Follow-up treatments are essential because cockroach eggs are encased in tough protective capsules (oothecae) that can withstand initial chemical applications. A scheduled follow-up ensures that any newly hatched nymphs are eradicated before they can mature and reproduce, breaking the biological lifecycle completely and securing long-term prevention.
