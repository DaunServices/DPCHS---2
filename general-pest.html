    General Pest Control Services Overview

    Daun Pest Control and Hygiene Services offers comprehensive solutions to eradicate and prevent stubborn pest infestations in residential, commercial, and industrial premises. Our general pest control service is specifically designed to ensure your living and working environments remain clean, safe, and free from dangerous disease vectors.

    Core Treatment Focus

    • Termites: Comprehensive inspections and structural protection systems to prevent high-value property damage.
    • Rodents (Rats & Mice): Population control utilizing strategic baiting and professional traps to break the breeding cycle.
    • Crawling Insects (Cockroaches, Ants, Bed Bugs): Effective chemical sprays and specialized gels to eliminate insects in hidden crevices.
    • Flying Insects (Flies, Mosquitoes): Thermal fogging techniques, Ultra Low Volume (ULV) spraying, and UV light trap installations.

    Our Service Advantages

    • Modern & Safe Technology: Utilization of the latest chemical formulations recognized by health authorities, alongside eco-friendly treatment techniques such as smokeless water-based thermal fogging.
    • Certified Team: Operated by experienced technicians who thoroughly understand local pest habits and biology in Malaysia.
    • Comprehensive Solutions: An integrated approach covering initial inspections, active eradication measures, and long-term prevention recommendations to avoid recurring issues.

    Frequently Asked Questions
    • What is General Pest Control?
      General Pest Control (GPC) is a foundational maintenance service focused on preventing, managing, and eliminating common household and commercial nuisance pests—primarily crawling insects—before they cause health risks or property damage.
    • What are the target pests?
      It covers common intruders such as ants, as well as German and American cockroaches. (Specialized pests like silverfish, spiders, beetles, subterranean termites, bed bugs, rodents, and mosquitoes typically require dedicated separate treatments).
    • What is the scope of treatment for general pest control?
      It involves targeted applications using residual liquid sprays, baits, and glue boards if necessary.
    • What is the primary objective?
      It suppresses low-level pest activity, maintains hygienic environments, and protects interior spaces from contamination or allergen spread on a routine schedule (such as monthly or quarterly).
