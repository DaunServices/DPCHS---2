    1. Overview

    Daun Pest Control And Hygiene Services delivers professional, reliable, and integrated pest management solutions tailored for both residential properties and commercial establishments throughout Selangor and Kuala Lumpur. Recognizing that a pest-free environment is essential for health, safety, and property preservation, we combine advanced treatment technologies with industry expertise to protect homes, offices, retail spaces, and industrial facilities from unwanted infestations.

    2. Core Treatment Focus

    • Residential Pest Control: Comprehensive interior and exterior treatments designed to safeguard families and homes from common household invaders such as ants, cockroaches, mosquitoes, rodents, and termites, ensuring a secure living space.
    • Commercial Pest Control: Customized, discreet, and regular maintenance programs for offices, restaurants, warehouses, and hospitality venues, strictly complying with hygiene standards and protecting business reputations.
    • Termite Management: Specialized pre-construction soil treatment and post-construction corrective systems, utilizing advanced baiting and liquid barrier techniques to protect structural integrity.
    • Thermal Fogging & Disinfection: Smokeless water-based thermal fogging and ULV misting for efficient mosquito vector control and holistic sanitization.

    3. Our Service Advantages

    • Experienced Technicians: Certified and well-trained professionals equipped with deep knowledge of local pest behaviors and eradication methods.
    • Safe & Regulated Solutions: Utilization of approved formulations at regulated dosages, prioritizing the safety of occupants and minimizing environmental risks.
    • Rapid Response: Prompt scheduling and efficient site assessment to address urgent pest outbreaks before they escalate.
    • Transparent Pricing & Guarantees: Competitive rates with clear service scopes and follow-up support to ensure long-term effectiveness.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    • 1. What is the difference between residential and commercial pest control services?
      Residential services focus on protecting families and homes from common household pests using targeted treatments for living spaces. Commercial services are designed for businesses like F&B outlets, offices, and warehouses, emphasizing compliance with health regulations, discreet execution, and preventative maintenance schedules.
    • 2. Are the chemical treatments safe for family members and pets?
      Yes, safety is our top priority. Although professional pest treatments involve active ingredients, we strictly use formulations approved by the Ministry of Health at regulated dosages. We apply targeted methods and advise occupants to stay out of treated zones until fully dry.
    • 3. How quickly can Daun Pest Control And Hygiene Services deploy a team for an urgent infestation?
      We offer rapid response times across Selangor and Kuala Lumpur. Typically, we can schedule an onsite inspection and treatment within 24 hours of your inquiry to prevent active infestations from escalating.
    • 4. What signs indicate that my property requires professional termite treatment?
      Key indicators include mud tubes running along walls, hollow-sounding timber when tapped, discarded wings near windowsills, or visible warping and bubbling in wooden flooring and door frames.
    • 5. How often should commercial premises schedule routine pest maintenance?
      Most commercial establishments, particularly F&B outlets, benefit from a monthly or bi-monthly service schedule. High-risk environments often require frequent monitoring to maintain strict hygiene compliance.
